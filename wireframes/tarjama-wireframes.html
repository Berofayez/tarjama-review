What happens next

  1. We check the file: type, readable Arabic text, 30 pages or fewer.
  2. The text is split into paragraphs.
  3. Each paragraph is translated with the church glossary applied. Terms like Old Man are required; Old Self is not allowed.
  4. Bible quotes are found in the Arabic and compared with the NKJV wording. The AI also marks phrases it was unsure about.
  5. You can start reviewing as soon as the first paragraphs are ready.

Translation speed is not a goal for the MVP. A 24-page book takes a few minutes.
